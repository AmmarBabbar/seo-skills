# Review Management

Reviews affect local rankings (prominence) and, even more, whether people call. You build a steady, honest review system and help the business reply to every review well.

## Before you start

1. Read `.agents/seo-context.md`.
2. Get: current review count and rating on Google and other key platforms, competitor numbers, how the business talks to customers after a job (SMS, email, in person), who will send requests.

## Compliance rules (never break these)

- No fake reviews, no buying reviews, no reviews written by staff or family
- No incentives for reviews (discounts, gifts, entries)
- No review gating: do not ask only happy customers or filter unhappy ones away from Google. Ask everyone.
- In the US, the FTC rule on fake reviews (2024) allows penalties for fake or bought reviews. Other countries have similar consumer laws.

## Getting more reviews

- **Timing**: ask right after the job is done and the customer is happy (same day)
- **Make it easy**: direct review link from GBP ("Ask for reviews" / share review form), QR code on invoices and leave-behind cards
- **Ask personally**: technicians ask in person, then a message follows within an hour
- **Follow up once** after 2-3 days if there is no review
- **Guide, don't script**: it is fine to ask customers to mention the service they got and their town, as long as you do not tell them what to say about quality

## Reply rules

- Reply to every review within 48 hours
- Use the reviewer's first name, mention the specific service naturally, thank them, keep it human
- Negative reviews: stay calm, acknowledge, do not argue or share private details, move the conversation offline with a name and phone number
- Fake or rule-breaking reviews: report them in GBP with the reason. Reply calmly in public that you have no record of this customer.

## Output

1. **Review request templates**: SMS (under 300 characters), email, in-person script, follow-up message
2. **Reply templates**: 5-star, 4-star with a small issue, 1-2 star complaint, suspected fake review
3. **Process**: who asks, when, with which tool, how it is tracked
4. **Targets**: realistic monthly review goal based on job volume (not on competitors alone)
5. **Showcasing**: how to display reviews on the website (real embeds or quotes). Do not add self-serving review star schema.
